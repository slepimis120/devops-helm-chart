Shop Operator je uspešno instaliran!

1. Čekaj da Deployment bude Ready:
   kubectl get deployment -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l app=shop-operator -w

2. Proveriti logs operatora:
   kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l app=shop-operator -f

3. Kreiraj prvi Shop resurs:
   kubectl apply -f config/samples/shop_v1_shop.yaml
